Executive Summary
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY · MARKET SPEND

The US specialty P&C actuarial workflow market is ~$750M / year, labor + consulting + tooling spend at 157 qualified carriers, with $9B+ of adjacent expansion behind it.

1
This is a spend-replacement market, not a SaaS-line TAM. The category addresses the labor, consulting, and tooling carriers already spend to do pricing and reserving. The number to size is that incumbent spend, not the SaaS line items vendors currently invoice.
2
US specialty P&C universe = 295 carriers, ~157 qualified. E&S (150) + specialty admitted (120) + reinsurers (25). After filters for in-house credentialed actuarial, GWP scale, and contractability: 157 buyable carriers.
3
Per-carrier spend is $4.5M / year. Avg 10 credentialed actuaries × $325K fully loaded = $3.25M labor, plus ~$800K consulting and ~$450K tooling. Across 157 carriers that totals ~$750M of annual workflow spend addressable to a specialty-actuarial software vendor.
4
Adjacent expansion brings the category to $9B+. US standard P&C, US life & health actuarial, big-4 consulting channel, global specialty, and adjacent modules each open with a discrete motion change.

Tesora prices on two axes: per-seat licenses for credentialed actuaries plus a usage fee on gross written premium (GWP) flowing through Tesora-built raters. The specialty P&C carrier with in-house pricing & reserving is the atomic unit of this TAM build.

Why "specialty carrier with in-house actuarial" is the right unit

Carriers that outsource pricing to consultants buy through that consultant, not directly. MGAs without credentialed actuaries buy through their fronting carrier. Captive insurers under $25M GWP can't justify six-figure tooling. Each of these is real volume, none of them is the unit being sized here.

PRICING ANCHOR · COMPETITIVE BAND
Generic AI assistants run ~$25K / yr for a 70-person team. Coding agents run ~$125K / yr for the same team. Tesora's "low six figures" sits in the middle of that band for a specialized actuarial workflow tool. We size at the floor of that band.
SEAT DENSITY · CALIBRATION
Mid-sized specialty carriers run 6–10 credentialed actuaries when capital, cat, and reserving are included. The pricing-and-reserving wedge addresses 3–8 of them; this analysis conservatively sizes at 4 seats per carrier.

Field pattern
Forward-leaning operators (1) describe intake and rate-filing as the actuary-hour drain, (2) flag the named comp set (Akur8, HX, Effective AI) as known but rigid or already deployed, (3) frame AI-native pricing as the differentiator against legacy carriers, not a marginal productivity gain. Implication for pursuit: head-on against Effective AI in this slice; wedge against rigidity for next-gen MGAs.
